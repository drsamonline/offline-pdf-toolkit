Photos only re-encodes the JPEGs inside the file and leaves the text as text. OCR not loaded

Reads text from scans. The engine is about 10 MB — two WASM cores and the English language data — and it ships beside this page, so loading it is not a download: it comes off your own disk, and your document never leaves the tab. It is only read when you press the button, which is why opening the app stays instant. Open Network above to watch that stay true.
